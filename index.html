01 / ABOUT MIYONI

A home for bold digital ideas.

Miyoni is an independent technology brand founded by Roosevelt Chikwende in Malawi.

It brings together a growing collection of digital products, each developed with its own purpose and identity.

From cloud infrastructure to education and communication, Miyoni is about turning ideas into practical digital experiences.

Our philosophy is simple: build with purpose, keep improving and think beyond borders.

Founder of Miyoni · Technology Builder · Malawi

Roosevelt Chikwende is the founder of Miyoni, an independent technology brand established to bring multiple digital product ideas under one identity.

His work centres on exploring ideas, developing software and building platforms that solve practical problems.

Through Miyoni, he is developing products in areas such as cloud services, education and communication.
