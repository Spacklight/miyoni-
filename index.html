01 / ABOUT MIYONI

A home for bold digital ideas.

Miyoni is an independent technology brand founded by Roosevelt Chikwende in Malawi.

It brings together a growing collection of digital products, each developed with its own purpose, identity and direction.

From cloud infrastructure to education and communication, Miyoni focuses on transforming ideas into practical digital experiences.

We believe technology should be useful, accessible and continuously improved.

Roosevelt Chikwende is the founder of Miyoni, an independent technology brand established to bring multiple digital product ideas under one identity.

His work centres on exploring ideas, developing software and building platforms that solve practical problems.

Through Miyoni, he is developing products across areas such as cloud services, education and communication.

His vision is to transform ideas into working digital experiences and build technology that can serve people beyond geographical boundaries.
